Is Oracle Cloud Free?
Yes, Oracle offers a Free Tier account.

Always Free Services: Autonomous Database, Compute VMs, Block Storage, Object Storage, Monitoring, and more.

Free Trial Credits: $300 worth of credits valid for 30 days to explore paid services.

After 30 days, if you don’t upgrade, you still keep the Always Free resources.
